You are at a joint between pages: the shell the map opens every page on, the
radio that one page lends another, the headers that let the site frame
itself, the middleware that makes two subdomains out of one project, or the
bindings. If the `architecture` skill is not already loaded, load it now
(`/architecture`, or `.claude/skills/architecture/SKILL.md`) and read
**Pages are documents, and the map is the shell** before changing anything
here. A change in `assets/` is also the `/site` process, and a change under
`functions/` or to `wrangler.toml` is also the `/api` process; this skill is
read with them, not instead of them.
